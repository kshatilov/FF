Hello. We are building the Shared light: A Framework for Collaborative Projective XR. Smart glasses and VR headsets have one interesting feature - they are extremely private. You never see what is happening inside the other person's HMD. And sometimes you need to see it - passing a meta quest to friend to check a cool XR statue, only to realize that the headset lost it's tracking and asking to reconfigure the boundary. Sometimes we need a shared spacio-digital canvas, such as when an artists draws a VR shape and people want to see it in real time through their personal devices, on the big screen behind and through their own perspective. These experiences are assymetric by nature, and we have to keep it in mind.
